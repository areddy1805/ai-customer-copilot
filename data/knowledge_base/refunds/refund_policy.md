## Refund Eligibility Policy

A customer is eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

- The order is cancelled before shipment
- The product is returned within 7 days of delivery
- The product is received in damaged or defective condition

## Refund Methods

- Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method
- Cash on Delivery (COD) orders are refunded as wallet credit

## Non-Refundable Cases

- Digital products
- Clearance sale items
- Products returned after 7 days
