## Delivery Delays

A delivery is considered delayed if it exceeds the estimated delivery window.

## Escalation Rules

- Delay > 3 days → customer eligible for support escalation
- Delay > 5 days → eligible for compensation or refund

## Actions

- Create support ticket
- Notify logistics partner
