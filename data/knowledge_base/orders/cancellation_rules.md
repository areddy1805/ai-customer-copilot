## Cancellation Rules

Orders can be cancelled only if they have not been shipped.

## Eligible States

- Placed
- Confirmed

## Non-Cancellable States

- Packed
- Shipped
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

## Refund After Cancellation

Refund is initiated immediately after successful cancellation.
