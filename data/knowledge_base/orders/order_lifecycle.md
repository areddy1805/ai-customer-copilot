## Order Lifecycle

An order progresses through the following states:

1. Placed
2. Confirmed
3. Packed
4. Shipped
5. Out for Delivery
6. Delivered

## Notes

- Cancellation is allowed only in Placed or Confirmed states
- Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled
